Nail Infection Treatment in Champapet, Hyderabad

Clear Thick, Discoloured Fungal Nails with Dermatologist-Led Care

Thick, yellow, brittle or crumbling nails are usually a sign of a fungal nail infection (onychomycosis) — one of the most common and stubborn nail problems. Nail infection treatment at V3 Derma Clinic, Champapet starts with confirming the diagnosis and then clearing the infection properly, because nail fungus does not go away on its own and tends to spread.

Because ordinary creams can't penetrate the hard nail, our dermatologist uses the right combination of prescription oral antifungals, medicated nail lacquers and nail care — with a plan to prevent it coming back. Early treatment gives the best chance of a clear, healthy nail.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to treat a fungal nail infection?

Nail infections clear slowly because the nail must grow out — usually 4–6 months for fingernails and 9–12 months for toenails. Consistency with treatment is key.

Why don't over-the-counter creams cure nail fungus?

Ordinary creams can't penetrate the hard nail plate to reach the fungus underneath. Nail infections usually need prescription oral antifungals, medicated lacquers or a combination.

Is a fungal nail infection contagious?

Yes — it can spread to other nails, to the skin, and to other people via shared footwear, towels and nail tools. Early treatment and good hygiene prevent spread.

Will my nail look normal again after treatment?

Usually yes, as the healthy nail grows out and the infection clears. Very long-standing or badly damaged nails may improve a lot but not always become perfectly normal.

Do I really need a doctor for nail fungus?

Yes — it doesn't clear on its own and tends to worsen and spread. A dermatologist confirms the diagnosis and prescribes effective treatment, which is especially important for people with diabetes.
