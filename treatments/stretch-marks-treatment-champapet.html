Frequently Asked Questions

Can stretch marks be removed completely?

They can be made much less visible, but complete removal is rarely possible. Early marks respond best; older white marks still improve.

Do stretch mark creams really work?

Creams mainly hydrate the surface; collagen-building treatments like micro-needling and laser work far better.

What is the best treatment for stretch marks?

A combination of micro-needling/MNRF and fractional laser, tailored by a dermatologist, usually gives the best improvement.

How many sessions will I need?

Most patients need a course of several sessions a few weeks apart, depending on extent and age of the marks.

When should I start treatment?

As early as possible — newer red or purple stretch marks respond significantly better than older ones.
