Frequently Asked Questions

What causes eczema and dermatitis?

Eczema results from a combination of a weak skin barrier, genetics and an overactive immune response, often triggered by allergens, irritants, weather or stress.

Can eczema be cured permanently?

Eczema is usually a chronic condition that cannot be permanently cured, but it can be very well controlled with the right treatment and skincare, with long symptom-free periods.

What are common eczema triggers?

Soaps and detergents, dust, certain fabrics, sweat, dry weather, stress and some foods can trigger flares. We help you identify and avoid yours.

Are over-the-counter creams enough for eczema?

Mild cases may improve with good moisturisers, but persistent or spreading eczema needs a dermatologist-prescribed plan to avoid steroid misuse and control flares.

When should I see a dermatologist for a rash?

See a dermatologist if a rash is very itchy, spreading, weeping, recurring or not improving with basic moisturising and care.
