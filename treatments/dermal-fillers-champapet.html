Frequently Asked Questions

What are dermal fillers made of?

Most modern dermal fillers use hyaluronic acid — a substance naturally found in the skin — making them safe, well-tolerated and reversible.

How long do dermal fillers last?

Depending on the product and area, results typically last from several months to over a year, after which a top-up can maintain them.

Does getting fillers hurt?

Discomfort is minimal. Fillers often contain a numbing agent, and a topical anaesthetic can be applied for comfort.

Are dermal fillers safe and reversible?

When administered by a qualified dermatologist, HA fillers are safe and can be dissolved if you are unhappy with the result.

When will I see results from fillers?

Results are visible immediately, with the final, settled look appearing within 1–2 weeks.
