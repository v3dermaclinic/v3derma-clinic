Frequently Asked Questions

What is microneedling RF?

Microneedling RF combines microneedles with radiofrequency energy to stimulate collagen and improve scars, pores, texture and mild sagging.

How many sessions of MNRF do I need?

Most patients need about 3–6 sessions around 4 weeks apart, depending on the concern.

Is MNRF painful?

A numbing cream is applied before treatment, so discomfort is usually mild and tolerable.

What is the downtime?

Redness and mild swelling usually last 1–3 days. Daily sunscreen and gentle skincare are important.

When will I see results?

Skin begins to improve within weeks and results continue building over 2–3 months as new collagen forms.
