Frequently Asked Questions

Are skin tags harmful?

Skin tags are almost always harmless. They are removed for comfort or cosmetic reasons, or if they catch on clothing or jewellery.

How are skin tags removed?

Skin tags are commonly removed by radiofrequency or cryotherapy under sterile conditions, quickly and with minimal discomfort.

Does skin tag removal hurt or scar?

With local numbing the procedure is comfortable, and techniques are chosen to minimise any scarring.

Will skin tags grow back after removal?

A removed skin tag does not usually grow back, though new tags can appear elsewhere over time, especially in prone areas.

Can I remove skin tags at home?

Home removal is not recommended — it risks bleeding, infection and scarring, and a growth should be confirmed as a harmless skin tag first.
