Types We Treat

Plaque Psoriasis

The most common type with thick, scaly patches.

Scalp Psoriasis

Itchy, flaky plaques on the scalp.

Nail Psoriasis

Pitting, discolouration and nail changes.

Guttate Psoriasis

Small, drop-shaped spots, often post-infection.

Inverse Psoriasis

Smooth red patches in skin folds.

Palmoplantar Psoriasis

Affecting the palms and soles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can psoriasis be cured permanently?

Psoriasis is a chronic condition that cannot be permanently cured, but it can be very effectively controlled — many patients achieve clear or nearly clear skin with proper treatment.

Is psoriasis contagious?

No. Psoriasis is not contagious; it is an immune-mediated condition and cannot be spread by touch.

What triggers psoriasis flare-ups?

Common triggers include stress, infections, cold weather, certain medications, skin injury, smoking and alcohol. We help you identify and manage yours.

What treatments are available for psoriasis?

Options include topical creams, phototherapy (NB-UVB), and systemic or biologic medication for more severe disease, chosen based on your case.

Do you treat scalp psoriasis?

Yes. We treat scalp psoriasis with medicated topicals, shampoos and, where needed, phototherapy and systemic options.
