Plasma Pen Treatment in Champapet, Hyderabad

Non-Surgical Skin Tightening & Growth Removal with Fibroblast Plasma

Want firmer, tighter skin or to remove a skin tag or mole without surgery? Plasma pen treatment at V3 Derma Clinic, Champapet — also called fibroblast therapy — uses a precise plasma arc to tighten skin and stimulate fresh collagen, with no cuts or stitches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is plasma pen treatment permanent?

Removing a skin tag or growth is usually permanent, while skin-tightening results last a long time but not forever, as natural aging continues. Maintenance can extend the effect.

Does plasma pen treatment hurt?

A numbing cream is applied first, so most patients feel only mild heat or tingling.

How long is the downtime?

Tiny carbon crusts form and flake off over about 5–7 days, with mild swelling possible for a few days.

Is plasma pen safe for Indian skin?

Performed by a dermatologist with correct settings and sun protection, it can be safe for Indian skin; aftercare is key to avoid pigmentation.

When will I see results?

Some tightening is immediate, with fuller collagen results developing over 4–8 weeks.
