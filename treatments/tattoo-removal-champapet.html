Frequently Asked Questions

How many sessions does laser tattoo removal take?

It varies with ink colour, density, depth and age of the tattoo — typically several sessions spaced 6–8 weeks apart, estimated at your consultation.

Does laser tattoo removal hurt?

There is some discomfort, often compared to a rubber-band snap; we take steps to keep you comfortable during the session.

Will tattoo removal leave a scar?

With a dermatologist using correct settings and proper aftercare, the risk of scarring is low.

Does laser remove all tattoo colours?

Black and dark inks respond best; some colours need more sessions. Your dermatologist will assess your tattoo.
