Frequently Asked Questions

What is a chemical peel and what does it do?

A chemical peel applies a medical solution that exfoliates dead and damaged skin cells, improving acne, pigmentation, dullness and fine lines as fresh skin emerges.

Which chemical peel is best for me?

It depends on your skin type and concern — salicylic for acne, glycolic for brightening, TCA for pigmentation. Your dermatologist recommends the right one at consultation.

Is there any downtime after a peel?

Most superficial peels have little to no downtime; you may see mild flaking for a few days. Stronger peels need a short recovery.

How many sessions of chemical peels are needed?

A course of 4–6 sessions every 2–4 weeks usually gives the best results, depending on your concern.

Are chemical peels safe and do they hurt?

Done by a dermatologist at correct strengths they are safe; you may feel a brief tingling or warmth during the peel.
