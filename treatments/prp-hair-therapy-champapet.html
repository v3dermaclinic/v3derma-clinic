PRP Hair Therapy in Champapet, Hyderabad

Natural Hair Regrowth Using Your Own Platelet-Rich Plasma

Worried about hair fall, thinning or a receding hairline? PRP hair therapy at V3 Derma Clinic, Champapet is a natural, non-surgical treatment that uses your own blood's growth factors to revive dormant follicles, reduce shedding and stimulate new growth.

A small blood sample is processed to concentrate platelet-rich plasma, which is injected into the scalp by our dermatologist. PRP is safe, has minimal downtime and works well for both men and women with early to moderate hair loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does PRP hair therapy work?

Your blood is spun in a centrifuge to concentrate platelet-rich plasma rich in growth factors, which is injected into the scalp to stimulate follicles, reduce hair fall and encourage regrowth.

How many PRP sessions are needed for hair?

Usually 4–6 sessions about 3–4 weeks apart, followed by maintenance, tailored to your degree of hair loss.

Is PRP hair treatment painful?

Discomfort is minimal; a numbing agent is applied and most patients tolerate it well.

When will I see results from PRP?

Reduced hair fall is often seen within weeks, with visible regrowth over 3–6 months.

Is PRP suitable for everyone?

PRP works best for early to moderate hair loss; a consultation determines if you are a good candidate.
