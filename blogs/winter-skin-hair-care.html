Winter Skin & Hair Care: Dry Skin, Eczema, Dandruff and Hair Fall

Quick answer: In winter, moisturise within 3 minutes of bathing, use lukewarm water, a soap-free cleanser and sunscreen, and treat dandruff with a medicated shampoo. See a dermatologist for severe itching, scaly patches or persistent hair fall.

As temperatures fall and humidity drops, many people in Hyderabad notice tight, flaky skin, itching, cracked heels and a flaky scalp. Winter changes your skin barrier — here is how to handle it.

Why Skin Gets Worse in Winter

Cold, dry air and hot showers strip natural oils, weakening the skin barrier. This worsens dryness, eczema, psoriasis and itching, and can make dandruff and hair fall more noticeable.

Winter Skincare Essentials

  • Moisturise within 3 minutes of bathing while skin is still damp.
  • Use lukewarm, not hot, water and keep showers short.
  • Choose a mild, soap-free cleanser and a ceramide-rich moisturiser.
  • Keep using sunscreen — UV does not disappear in winter.
  • Moisturise hands, lips and heels regularly.

Winter Hair & Scalp Care

  • Wash hair with lukewarm water and avoid over-washing.
  • Treat dandruff with a suitable medicated shampoo instead of just oiling.
  • Avoid heat styling and tight hairstyles that increase breakage.
  • Persistent shedding for more than 2–3 months needs a dermatologist's evaluation.

When to See a Dermatologist

  • Intense itching, red scaly patches or cracked, bleeding skin
  • Dandruff that returns quickly or with hair thinning
  • Suspected eczema, psoriasis or fungal infection

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my skin get so dry in winter?

Cold, low-humidity air and hot showers remove natural oils and weaken the skin barrier, causing dryness, tightness and itching.

Can winter cause hair fall and dandruff?

Yes. Dry air and a dry scalp can increase flaking and breakage. Persistent hair fall should be evaluated by a dermatologist.

Do I still need sunscreen in winter?

Yes. UVA rays are present year-round, so daily sunscreen is still recommended.

When should I see a dermatologist for winter skin problems?

See a dermatologist for severe itching, scaly red patches, cracked bleeding skin or dandruff and hair fall that do not improve with basic care.
