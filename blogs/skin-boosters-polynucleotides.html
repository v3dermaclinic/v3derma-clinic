Skin Boosters & Polynucleotides: The New Glow Treatment Explained

Quick answer: Skin boosters are hyaluronic-acid micro-injections that hydrate skin and improve glow and elasticity; polynucleotides are DNA-fragment injectables for skin repair. Both need a dermatologist's assessment — available at V3 Derma Clinic, Champapet, Hyderabad.

"Glass skin" and "skin quality" are the most searched skin goals today, and skin boosters — including newer polynucleotide (PN) injectables — are the treatments behind the trend. Unlike fillers that add volume, boosters improve the skin itself: hydration, elasticity, texture and glow.

What Are Skin Boosters?

Skin boosters are tiny injections of non-cross-linked hyaluronic acid (HA) placed in the skin's upper layers. They draw in and hold water, so skin looks plumper, smoother and more radiant from within. They are different from dermal fillers, which restructure or add volume.

What Are Polynucleotides?

Polynucleotides are purified DNA fragments used in regenerative aesthetics. They are used to support skin repair, improve hydration and elasticity, and are popular for fine lines, thin crepey skin and the delicate under-eye area. Suitability depends on your skin and goals, so a dermatologist should assess you first.

Who Can Benefit

  • Dull, dehydrated or tired-looking skin
  • Fine lines and early signs of aging (late 20s onward)
  • Rough texture and enlarged-looking pores
  • Skin that looks thin or lacks bounce
  • Anyone wanting a glow before a wedding or event

What to Expect

  • Procedure: A numbing cream is applied, then micro-injections are placed across the face, neck or hands. It takes about 30–45 minutes.
  • Downtime: Small bumps and redness usually settle within a day or two; mild bruising can occur.
  • Sessions: Most protocols use a short series of sessions spaced a few weeks apart, then maintenance. Your dermatologist plans this for you.
  • Results: Hydration and glow build gradually over the following weeks.

Safety Matters

Choose a qualified dermatologist, genuine sealed products and a sterile setup. Boosters are not suitable during active skin infections, pregnancy or breastfeeding, or if you have certain allergies — always share your medical history.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between skin boosters and fillers?

Skin boosters hydrate and improve skin quality using fine hyaluronic acid injections, while dermal fillers add volume and shape to areas like cheeks or lips.

What are polynucleotides in skin treatment?

Polynucleotides are purified DNA-fragment injectables used in regenerative aesthetics to support skin repair, hydration and elasticity.

Is there downtime after skin boosters?

Downtime is minimal. Small bumps and redness usually settle within one to two days, and mild bruising may occur.

How many skin booster sessions do I need?

Most plans use a short series of sessions a few weeks apart followed by maintenance, tailored by your dermatologist.
