Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our dermatology clinic in Champapet, Hyderabad

Where is V3 Derma Clinic located?

V3 Derma Clinic is located at 1st Floor, Shobha Enclave, Green Park Colony, beside HDFC Bank, Champapet Road, Hyderabad – 500079.

What are the clinic's working hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM, and Sunday from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

How do I book an appointment at V3 Derma Clinic?

You can book an appointment by calling 8897489595, messaging us on WhatsApp, or filling out the appointment form on our Contact page.

What treatments does V3 Derma Clinic offer?

We offer clinical dermatology (acne, psoriasis, eczema, vitiligo, pigmentation), hair treatments (PRP therapy, hair-fall and alopecia treatment), laser treatments (hair reduction, scar and tattoo removal, skin resurfacing) and cosmetic procedures (chemical peels, anti-aging, dermal fillers and HydraFacial).

Who will treat me at V3 Derma Clinic?

Treatments are carried out by a qualified consultant dermatologist, cosmetologist and trichologist following evidence-based, international dermatology protocols.

Does V3 Derma Clinic offer laser hair reduction?

Yes. We offer US-FDA approved diode laser hair reduction for the face and body, along with other advanced laser treatments such as scar removal, tattoo removal and skin resurfacing.

Who is the dermatologist at V3 Derma Clinic in Champapet?

Dr. O Lakshmi Jhansi, a consultant dermatologist, cosmetologist and trichologist, leads V3 Derma Clinic. She treats acne, pigmentation, hair loss and skin conditions using evidence-based, international dermatology protocols.

Which areas does V3 Derma Clinic serve near Champapet?

Along with Champapet and Green Park Colony, we are conveniently located for patients from Saroornagar, BN Reddy Nagar, Santoshnagar, Lalitha Nagar, Jillelaguda, Karmanghat, Kothapet and Dilsukhnagar in Hyderabad.
